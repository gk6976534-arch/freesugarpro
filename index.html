Eleuthero

Eleuthero, also known as Siberian ginseng, is a botanical ingredient traditionally used in wellness preparations. It is commonly associated with energy, stamina, and resilience to everyday fatigue. However, evidence supporting its specific effects on blood glucose levels remains limited.
3

Maca Root

Maca root comes from a plant native to the Andes Mountains of Peru. It is traditionally used in foods and supplements intended to support vitality and general well-being. Although researchers have investigated maca for several metabolic effects, there is insufficient clinical evidence to conclude that it meaningfully regulates blood sugar in humans.
4

Chromium

Chromium is an essential trace mineral involved in normal nutrient metabolism. It has been studied for its potential role in carbohydrate metabolism and insulin function. Research on chromium supplementation and blood glucose control has produced mixed results, and the benefits may depend on an individual's nutritional status and other factors.
5

Ginseng

Ginseng, particularly Asian or Panax ginseng, is a botanical ingredient traditionally used to support general wellness. Researchers have investigated its potential effects on glucose metabolism, but findings are not conclusive enough to establish it as a treatment for diabetes. The specific type and amount used in a supplement are important considerations.
6

African Mango

African mango (Irvingia gabonensis) is a fruit native to parts of West and Central Africa. Its seed extract is used in some supplements marketed for metabolic wellness and weight management. Research remains limited, and more rigorous clinical trials are needed to establish its effectiveness for blood sugar regulation.
